Common Questions

Everything you want to know

How does it work?
Fill out the intake form with your story. Tim writes a fully produced, custom song around your memories — heartfelt, one-of-a-kind, and ready to share forever. After submitting, you'll be contacted within 24 hours by email with next steps.
How fast will I get my song?
Most songs are delivered within 48–72 hours. Memorial songs are always delivered within 24 hours at no extra cost. For any other song on a tighter timeline, rush delivery is available for an additional $50 — just mention your date on the intake form and Tim will confirm what's possible.
Can I pick the style or mood?
Absolutely. You choose the genre and vibe — romantic, funny, emotional, nostalgic, solemn — whatever fits your story best.
Can I approve the lyrics?
Yes. You'll receive the lyrics first with one round of revisions before production begins.
Can I give this as a gift?
Absolutely — a custom song makes one of the most personal and memorable gifts you can give. Many clients order songs as wedding gifts, birthday surprises, anniversary presents, or to honor someone they love. Your personal keepsake page and downloads are yours to share however you choose — privately or in front of a room full of people.
How do I share my song once it's delivered?
When your song is finished, you'll get an email with the link to your personal keepsake page — the home base where everything lives: your song on an unlisted YouTube link, the MP3 and lyric sheet to download and keep, and the album art and full lyrics displayed on the page. Unlisted means the song never shows up in search or public listings — only people you share the link with will find it. Share it by text, email, or social media — anyone you send it to can watch and listen instantly — or download the MP3 and play it at an event, or keep it just for family.
Is my song really one-of-a-kind?
Always. Every song is built from scratch — no templates, no shortcuts. Your story is yours alone.
